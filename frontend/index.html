    The 14-agent pipeline

    1

    Data Collector

    Pulls financials, news, peers, insider trades, and price history from Financial Modeling Prep.

    2

    Classifier

    Identifies stock type (Value, Growth, Optionality, etc.) and picks the right valuation framework.

    3

    Fundamentals

    Business model, moat, margin trajectory analysis.

    4

    Catalyst Scout

    Real catalysts from news + upcoming earnings dates.

    5

    Peer Analyst

    Pulls 3-5 peers, builds comp table, interprets premium/discount.

    6

    Technicals

    Reads price action, momentum, and proximity to 52w range.

    7

    Macro Strategist

    Sector cycle, rate environment, top-down call.

    8

    DCF Modeler

    5-year DCF anchored to historical FCF margins. Python deterministic math.

    9

    Comps Modeler

    Applies peer median multiples to subject's financials.

    10

    Scenarios

    Bull/Base/Bear cases with explicit assumption flexing.

    11

    Reality Check

    Weights methods by stock type. De-weights DCF when it gives nonsense.

    12

    Bull Advocate

    Aggressive long case. Adversarial role.

    13

    Bear Advocate

    Aggressive short case. Adversarial role.

    14

    Chief Strategist

    Synthesizes everything, makes the final call, writes the report.

    Built with LangGraph, Gemini 2.5 Flash, FastAPI, and Financial Modeling Prep. For educational use only — not investment advice.
